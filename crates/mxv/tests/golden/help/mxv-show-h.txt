Show workspaces by name, in the order given

Usage: mxv show [OPTIONS] <NAME>...

Arguments:
  <NAME>...  The names of the workspaces to show, separated by spaces

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  mxv show reading-list

Effects: read-only, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full, --no-input. `mxv show --help` describes them.
Next: mxv show --help explains each column.

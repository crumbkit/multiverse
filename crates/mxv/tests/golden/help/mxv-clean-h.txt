Delete the disposable directories, such as target/ and node_modules/, of idle workspaces

Usage: mxv clean [OPTIONS] <NAME|--all|--over-limit>

Arguments:
  [NAME]...  The names of the workspaces to clean, separated by spaces

Options:
      --all         Clean every idle workspace
      --over-limit  Clean idle workspaces, oldest first, until the disposable directories of every workspace take no more than the config file's max_disposable
      --idle <AGE>  How long a checkout must go unmodified to count as idle, such as 7d, 36h or 0s; it defaults to the config file's clean_idle, or else 7d
      --disposable  With --yes, also delete the paths that only mxv.toml declares disposable
      --dry-run     Report what the command would change, and change nothing
      --yes         Proceed without asking for confirmation
  -h, --help        Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  mxv clean --all --dry-run

Effects: destructive, idempotent, closed-world.
Writes: reports each item and exits 1 when any item fails.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full, --no-input. `mxv clean --help` describes them.

Rebuild an archived workspace at its archived commits

Usage: mxv restore [OPTIONS] <NAME>

Arguments:
  <NAME>  The name of the archived workspace

Options:
      --force        Restore without the ignored files whose copy in the archive is missing or damaged, and list them as lost
      --from-origin  Fetch origin into each clone's mirror, making the mirror again when it is gone or has lost its pins, and restore only what origin holds; this is the only option that opens a network connection
      --dry-run      Report what the command would change, and change nothing
      --yes          Proceed without asking for confirmation
  -h, --help         Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  mxv restore sample --from-origin --dry-run

Effects: additive, idempotent, open-world.
Global flags: --json, --no-input. `mxv restore --help` describes them.
